========================
Advanced lesson settings
========================

In this article, you will learn how to adjust the advanced lesson settings.

Before publishing an exploration, make sure you've filled out all the necessary
fields in the **Settings** page. To learn more, go to
:doc:`/creators/lesson-setup`. 

.. _adjust_advanced_lesson_settings_ref:

To adjust the advanced lesson settings:

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Select **Advanced Features**.

Email preferences
-----------------

By default, you'll receive email notifications when a learner submits feedback
or a suggestion on your exploration.

To change your preferences:

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Feedback/Suggestion email preferences**, use the toggle to turn 
   notifications on or off.
