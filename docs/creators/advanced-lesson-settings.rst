.. _enter_basic_settings_ref:

Enter basic settings
====================

Before publishing an exploration, make sure you’ve filled out all the necessary 
fields on the **Settings** page. To learn more, go to 
:doc:`/creators/lesson-setup`. 

.. _adjust_advanced_lesson_settings_ref:

Adjust advanced lesson settings
===============================

Advanced features are located in the **Advanced Features** section in **Settings**.

Automatic text-to-speech
------------------------

Automatic text-to-speech is enabled by default and automatically generates audio 
from the text in each card.

When text-to-speech is enabled, learners will see an **audio button** while 
playing the exploration. Selecting it will play a computerized reading of the 
card’s content. 

.. note::
   Disable the text-to-speech feature if your exploration content uses multiple 
   languages.

.. figure:: /images/creators/lesson_settings_advanced.png
   :alt: The Advanced Features section of the lesson settings page showing the toggle for automatic text-to-speech.

   *Fig. 1. Lesson Settings in the Exploration Editor*

Email preferences
-----------------

By default, you’ll receive email notifications when a learner submits feedback 
or a suggestion on your exploration.

To change your preferences:

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Feedback/Suggestion email preferences**, use the toggle to turn 
   notifications on or off.

.. figure:: /images/creators/feedback_email_preferences.png
   :alt: The email preferences section in settings showing the toggle switch for feedback and suggestion notifications.

   *Fig. 2. Feedback email preferences in Lesson settings*
